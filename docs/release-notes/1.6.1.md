### "⌘O" stays with its line

Adding JPEG XL to the list of formats under **Drag files here** made the line
too long for a new window, and "⌘O" wrapped onto a line of its own. The list
is shorter now, and wherever the line still has to wrap, "or press ⌘O" moves
down as one piece instead of leaving "⌘O" stranded.
