### Meet Shrinker Studio

Shrinker Pro stays free and unlimited. Studio is an optional upgrade inside
it, for the photos that need more than a quick squeeze:

- **Compare before you save:** split, side by side, or a Difference view that
  shows every pixel compression changed.
- **Recipes:** resize and crop, adjust, add a look, a watermark, a border or
  new names, then save it for any batch.
- **Watch folders:** new photos in a folder come out finished, even with no
  window open.

Press **Studio** in the toolbar for a tour. Every Studio tool works for your
first 10 exports; a one-time licence unlocks the rest.

### Also fixed

- The main window opens at its own size at launch.
- The settings button can be read by VoiceOver and the overflow menu.
