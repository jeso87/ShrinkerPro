**Set a maximum size.** Type a number at the bottom of the window and nothing comes out bigger than that on its longest side. Pictures keep their proportions, and anything already smaller is left alone.

**The bottom of the window now says what will happen** to the next files you drop — the format, the quality and the size limit, all on one line. Click **Adjust** to change them. These apply until you quit; your usual settings stay as they are.

**Shrinker Pro asks before replacing a file.** If a file is about to be written over, you choose: skip it, keep both, or replace it.

**Settings is easier to read**, grouped into clearly labelled sections.
