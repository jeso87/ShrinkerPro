### Shrinker Studio

- **Straighten.** A new recipe step levels a tilted horizon automatically. On any photo, Straighten by hand… sets the angle yourself against a grid.
- **Keep the whole photo.** For stamps, prints and anything whose edges matter: nothing is cut off, and the corners are filled with the background or a colour you pick.
- **Crop to the item.** Studio levels a scan and crops it to the stamp, print or card on the scanner glass, with a margin from none to 10%. Dust and hairs on the glass are left out.
- **Side by side fits beside the sidebars,** and you can zoom out until even a very large scan fits.
- **Fixed:** with All photos chosen, dragging the quality slider in Looks the same changed only the photo in front. It now sets the recipe to Manual at that quality, so it saves with the recipe.
