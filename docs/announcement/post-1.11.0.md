Hi everyone,

Shrinker Pro 1.11.0 is out. This is the biggest Studio update so far: photos turn themselves upright, backgrounds come off on your Mac, and Studio can check every file against the rules of the place you're sending it.

**[Download Shrinker Pro](https://github.com/jeso87/ShrinkerPro/releases/latest/download/ShrinkerPro.dmg)**. Already have it? Choose **Check for Updates…**, or `brew upgrade --cask shrinker-pro`.

## Rotate, in both apps

In the free app, right-click a result and choose **Rotate Left** or **Rotate Right**. The result is made again from your original, so turning it doesn't cost any quality.

In Studio, the Straighten step is now **Rotate & straighten**. Turn photos a quarter or half turn, or flip them, for the whole recipe or just the selected photos (⌘L and ⌘R). Levelling the horizon is still there.

## Turn each photo upright

Studio looks at faces and text to decide which way is up, and turns each photo to match. If a photo isn't clear either way, it's left alone. On my test set of 232 photos it got 228 right and none wrong. **Rotate Upright** (⇧⌘U) does it for just the selection.

## Remove background

Add **Remove background** to a recipe and the subject is cut out right on your Mac, with nothing uploaded. Put it on white, black, any colour or transparency, with a soft or contact shadow if you like. This one needs macOS 15 or later.

If you sell online, white has to be really white. With a white backdrop, Studio checks the saved JPEG or PNG and keeps it at exactly 255, 255, 255.

Products already shot on near-white? **Only whiten the backdrop** makes the backdrop pure white and never touches the product.

## Check for a destination

Studio now knows the published rules for 21 destinations: marketplaces like Amazon, Etsy, eBay and Shopify, social networks, camera clubs, stock libraries and print sizes. Every rule has its source and the date I checked it.

Choose **Start from a Destination** and the whole recipe is set up for it. The **Check for** step marks every file Pass, Warn or Fail with a plain reason. It never blocks an export, it just tells you.

Two new tools came along with it:

- **Frame the subject:** the subject fills a set share of the frame, like the 85% that Amazon's main image calls for.
- **Fit inside:** fit every photo in a box, such as a club projector's 1920 × 1080.

## Also new

- **Rounded borders** can round the outside too, with clear corners.
- **New social crops:** Instagram grid, Threads, TikTok photo and Facebook square. Facebook post is now 1080 × 1350.
- **Shortcuts:** Rotate Images and Remove Background.
- The free app's drop area now lists every format it reads, and a rare stall with big batches is fixed.

Shrinker Pro is still free. Studio comes with it, every tool works for your first 10 exports, and a one-time licence is at [shrinkerstudio.app](https://shrinkerstudio.app).
