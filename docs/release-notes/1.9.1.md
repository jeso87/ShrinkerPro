### Shrinker Studio

- **Sharpen is a full unsharp mask.** Amount up to 400%, plus Radius and Threshold. Radius matches Affinity's Unsharp Mask, so your Affinity settings carry over. The Shortcuts action has them too.
- **Rounded corners without a frame.** Set a Rounded border's width to 0% to round just the photo's corners.
- **Save to ▸ Ask when exporting.** Pick the folder each time you export.
- **Type any slider's value** in a step's popover.
