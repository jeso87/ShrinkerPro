A small fix to 1.6.0: "⌘O" no longer wraps onto a line of its own in the
drop zone.

**[ShrinkerPro-1.6.1.dmg](https://github.com/jeso87/ShrinkerPro/releases/download/v1.6.1/ShrinkerPro-1.6.1.dmg)** — 5.8 MB. Open it and drag Shrinker Pro to Applications.

## Fixed

Adding JXL to the drop zone's format line in 1.6.0 pushed it past the width of
a new window, so "⌘O" wrapped alone onto a second line. The English line drops
its "and" — "…GIF, SVG — or press ⌘O" — and fits on one line again at the
default size. "or press ⌘O" is held together by no-break spaces, so a narrower
window moves the whole phrase down rather than stranding "⌘O".

Every translation does the same with its own wording. Most are long enough to
wrap at the default width regardless, so the glue is what fixes them: checked
by launching the build in all 36 languages, eighteen fit on one line and the
rest break after the dash with the phrase whole.

Nothing else changed. The `shrinker` command is rebuilt at 1.6.1 to keep its
version in step with the app, and behaves identically to 1.6.0.
