### Shrinker Pro

- **Converting to WebP, AVIF or JPEG XL never makes a file bigger.** If the
  result would be larger than the original, the original is kept instead.
- **AVIF at quality 100 works again** on macOS 27.
- **What's New** in the Shrinker Pro menu shows these notes for the last few
  versions.

### Shrinker Studio

- **35 looks**, including a new Brand duotone in your own two colours. The
  looks you star sit at the top.
- **A zoom control** in the corner of the photo, and the Studio window can go
  full screen.
- **More room for the photo:** drag the photo list's edge to resize it, or
  fold it away with ⌃⌘S. The handle on the Split divider shows only when
  your pointer is near it.
- **Glass sidebars**, if you like: turn them on in Settings ▸ Studio.
- **Resize by the shortest side**, and choose whether the app opens in
  Shrinker Pro or straight into Studio.
- **Previews no longer get stuck** when you switch photos while they update,
  and a photo whose preview will refresh when you switch to it is marked in
  the list.
- **Write a review** from the Shrinker Pro menu, if you'd like to tell other
  people what Studio does for you.
