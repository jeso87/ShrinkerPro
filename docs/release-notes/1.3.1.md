**Settings says what it actually does.** The choice about `.min` used to read "Keep originals, save a .min copy" or "Replace originals" no matter where your files were going. If you send them to another folder, or to a "minified" subfolder, nothing of yours can be replaced — so it now simply asks whether to add `.min`, and shows you the filename either way. Where your files go, and what they are called, has not changed.

**Shrinker Pro now checks for updates once a day, and the setting for it is gone.** This app is free and open source, and most releases are here because somebody asked for what is in them — so an install that never hears about a new version is the thing worth fixing.

Checking is not installing. You are still told about a new version and still decide whether to take it, and **Check for Updates…** in the menu works as before.
