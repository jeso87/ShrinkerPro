### Shrinker Pro

- **Rotate Left and Rotate Right.** Right-click a result to turn it. It's made again from the original, so turning it never costs quality.
- **Every format it reads** is now listed where you drop files.
- **Steadier with big batches.** A rare stall while compressing many files at once is fixed.

### Shrinker Studio

- **Rotate & straighten.** Turn photos a quarter or half turn, or flip them, for the whole recipe or just the selected photos (⌘L and ⌘R), as well as levelling the horizon.
- **Turn each photo upright.** Faces and text decide which way is up. A photo where that isn't clear is left alone. Rotate Upright does it for the selection.
- **Remove background** (macOS 15 or later). The subject is cut out on your Mac and put on white, black, a colour or transparency, with an optional shadow. A white backdrop stays exactly 255, 255, 255 in JPEG and PNG.
- **Only whiten the backdrop**, for products already shot on near-white: the product is never touched.
- **Check for a destination.** 21 marketplaces, social networks, camera clubs, stock libraries and print sizes. Start a recipe from one, and every file is marked pass, warn or fail against its published rules.
- **Frame the subject** so it fills a set share of the frame, and **Fit inside** a box such as a club projector's 1920 × 1080.
- **Rounded borders** can round the outside too, with clear corners.
- **New social crops:** Instagram grid, Threads, TikTok photo and Facebook square. Facebook post is now 1080 × 1350.
- **Shortcuts:** Rotate Images and Remove Background.
